The Shift from AI to Coding

When I first discovered AI, I thought I had found a shortcut to building the web. I would simply ask ChatGPT to generate code, copy it, and paste it. It worked, but I felt like I was looking at a language I couldn't actually speak. I realized that to truly create, I needed to understand the foundation myself.

That is when I started my journey on Coddy. Instead of letting AI do the heavy lifting, I now use it as a guide to help me navigate the basics. I'm moving away from just "generating" and moving toward "building" with my own hands.

By breaking down the code line by line, I'm finally learning how to structure a page correctly. It’s a challenge, but seeing my own code come to life is much more satisfying than just hitting copy and paste.
